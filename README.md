## Birdeye Extractor

**Birdeye Extractor** is a data extraction tool designed to collect **publicly available business information** from Birdeye across **all categories**, including healthcare providers, contractors, home services, restaurants, retail, automotive, and more. The tool helps organize business listings into structured datasets for research, analysis, and lead discovery.

### Supported Categories

* Healthcare (clinics, dentists, doctors, therapists)
* Contractors (construction, plumbing, electrical, HVAC)
* Home Services
* Restaurants & Food Services
* Automotive Services
* Retail & Local Businesses
* Professional Services
* And other publicly listed categories

### Extracted Data

* Business name
* Category / industry
* Address & location
* Phone number
* Website (if available)
* Ratings & review count
* Other public metadata

### Features

* Works with **multiple business categories**
* Extracts data from **public Birdeye listings**
* Structured output (CSV / JSON)
* Easy to customize and extend
* Suitable for research, analytics, and educational projects

### Use Cases

* Market & competitor research
* Local business analysis
* Lead generation (B2B research)
* Data science and scraping practice
